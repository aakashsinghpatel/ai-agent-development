These are general study notes for organizing important information clearly and efficiently.
 Good notes should be simple, structured, and easy to review later. 
 Start by writing the main topic, followed by important points, examples, definitions, and 
 conclusions. Use headings and bullet points to separate different ideas. 
 Avoid unnecessary details and focus on information that helps with understanding the subject.
  Reviewing notes regularly improves memory and makes exam preparation easier. 
  You can also highlight keywords or create short summaries after each section.
